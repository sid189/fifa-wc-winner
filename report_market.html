How to use this

  1. Big positive diffs are either alpha or bugs. Cross-check against compare_models.py and backtest_all.py before sizing a bet.
  2. Big negative diffs are market signals. The market typically knows about current form, injuries, recent friendly results that pre-tournament Elo has not absorbed.
  3. Devigging method. Proportional devig used here. For high-margin books switch to Shin's method in src/market.py.

Refresh the odds

Replace CURRENT_ODDS at the top of report_market.py with current decimal odds from Pinnacle, Betfair Exchange, or oddsportal.com.
